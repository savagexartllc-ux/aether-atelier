Nigredo — About

The Alchemist

I work in layers: gesture, resin and colour, transmuted until a space has something to say. Every piece is made as an original, or as a commission for a particular energy.

Beyond the canvas, my practice reaches into live painting at events, paint-release sessions for wellness gatherings, private lessons, and palette & art consulting for the home.

Our Belief

Art should change a space the way weather changes the mood.

Every piece begins with a conversation about where it will live, your palette, and how you want it to feel.
