The Imprint

Private Body Print Sessions

Your form, stamped in colour: a one-of-a-kind portrait made without a brush. In a private session, paint is applied to the front or back of your body, or to you and your partner together, and you are guided onto fresh canvas so the shape of you becomes the artwork.

We plan the position together beforehand: standing, curved, reclined or embracing. Every print is unrepeatable. Framed, it is a bold boudoir gift, an anniversary piece, or a celebration of your own body to hang for yourself.

  • Private, by appointment only, with just the artist. No assistants, no onlookers. Your comfort sets the pace. Choose front, back, partial or draped, and we agree on everything before paint touches skin.
  • Solo or couple. One body, or two together on a single canvas.
  • Body-safe paints. Cosmetic-grade, washable body paint, never craft paint on skin.
  • Gift certificates available. Anniversaries, weddings, birthdays and self-love milestones.

Privacy & Consent

Every Imprint session is a private session between you (and your partner, for couples) and me, the artist. No one else is present. Before each session we review a short consent form and you sign a release confirming what we have agreed: the positioning, the paint used, and how your print and information will be handled. Sessions are for adults 18 and over only.

No photographs or video are taken unless you ask for them in writing. Your name, your print and your details are never displayed, posted or shared without your written permission, and the information you give is used only to schedule and complete your session. You can pause or stop at any point.

Couple from $750
